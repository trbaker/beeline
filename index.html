Beeline

Fly your honeybee through real city blocks, from a tiny 2 × 3 grid up to 5 × 6, and gather pollen from every flower. Watch out for the wasps buzzing around the streets!
Find a super flower in each corner. It makes your bee giant for a few seconds, so you can bonk the wasps back to their nest. Arrow keys or WASD on a keyboard; swipe or use the pad on a phone. Zoom with the + and − buttons, the mouse wheel or a pinch. Visit every flower to fly to the next city.
Difficulty
Standard speed, 3 lives
